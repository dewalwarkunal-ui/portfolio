About Me

Turning complexity into clarity, structure and scale.

I started in architecture, moved into design and client delivery, and gradually became more interested in the systems behind the work — how teams were organised, how decisions were made, how work moved between people, and where things broke down.

That path took me from design into delivery, then into operations, program management and transformation.

Today, I work at the intersection of people, process and technology — helping organisations bring structure to complexity without creating unnecessary bureaucracy.

My Approach

Understand. Stabilize. Transform.

I don't start with the solution. I start by understanding what is actually happening.

01

Understand

See the system before changing it.

I look beyond the visible problem to understand the people, processes, data, technology and decisions around it.

  • Reality
  • Root causes
  • Ownership
  • Evidence
02

Stabilize

Create clarity before creating change.

Once the problem is understood, I create the structure needed to make work predictable and accountable.

  • Roles & ownership
  • Governance
  • Standards
  • Visibility
03

Transform

Change the system, not just the symptom.

With a stable foundation in place, I redesign processes, introduce technology and automate where it creates meaningful value.

  • Process redesign
  • Systems transformation
  • Workflow automation
  • Operating model design
Understand the problem. Stabilize the system. Transform what needs to change.

Leadership

Structure over supervision.

I believe leadership is less about controlling the work and more about creating the conditions in which good work can happen.

01

Build teams

Give people clarity, context and ownership — then give them room to perform.

02

Manage conflict

Look for the system creating the conflict, not just the people involved in it.

03

Work with stakeholders

Make complexity understandable without hiding the detail that matters.

04

Prioritise

Make priorities visible so teams know what matters now, what can wait and why.

05

Make decisions

Use evidence wherever possible, but don't wait for perfect information when a decision is needed.

06

Drive change

Change sticks when people understand why it matters and how their day-to-day work will change.
