About

Origin

From architecture to enterprise operations

I began my career as an architect, where I learned that great outcomes depend as much on execution as they do on design. That perspective gradually led me from designing physical spaces to designing the systems, processes, and operating models that enable teams and organizations to perform at their best. Over the course of my career, I have managed more than 150 client engagements, leading cross-functional teams, strengthening governance, and driving operational excellence.

Today, I lead operations for Atlas Copco's 70-member in-house Brand Studio in India, supporting business units across the globe. My focus is on building scalable operations through process transformation, workflow optimization, data-driven decision-making, and AI-enabled ways of working. A Master's in Strategic Design Management from NID Ahmedabad, with a collaborative programme at IIM Ahmedabad, strengthened my systems thinking and continues to shape how I approach leadership, transformation, and organizational growth.

Approach

How I solve problems

I believe effective problem solving begins with understanding, not assumptions. Before recommending changes, I take the time to understand the current state by engaging with stakeholders, analyzing processes, and identifying where challenges originate. Rather than addressing issues in isolation, I examine how operations, processes, communication, accountability, financial management, and data interact within the broader system. This enables me to identify root causes and implement solutions that deliver meaningful, sustainable improvements.

Leadership

Structure over supervision.

How I build teams, manage stakeholders, and drive change — in practice, not in theory.

01

Clarity

People perform better when they know what success looks like. I establish clear roles, ownership, priorities, and expectations before asking teams to execute.

02

Visibility

I believe leaders should be able to see the health of the operation without creating a reporting burden for the team. Good dashboards replace unnecessary meetings.

03

Accountability

Accountability works when ownership is explicit and measurable. I use RACI, KPIs, operating rhythms, and escalation mechanisms to make accountability practical.

04

Change

Transformation is a people problem as much as a process or technology problem. I involve teams early, communicate the why, and build adoption into the change itself.

05

Continuous Improvement

I look for small, measurable improvements that compound. Not every problem needs a major transformation programme; sometimes the best solution is a better workflow.

06

People First

Processes exist to support people, not the other way around. I focus on creating an environment where teams have the clarity, tools, and support they need to do their best work.
